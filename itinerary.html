Which pass

Buy the Explorer pass with 6 choices for you, your husband, your parents and your younger brother. It activates on first use (Mon Oct 12) and lasts 30 days, so the Friday Statue trip is still covered, and nothing has to be crammed into consecutive days. Spend the choices on the expensive tickets: 9/11 Museum, Empire State, Top of the Rock, AMNH, Statue/Ellis, Circle Line. Pay separately for the cheap extras (Butterbeer, Rink, Picnic).

An All-Inclusive pass only wins if you do 3+ paid attractions every day on consecutive days. Your only two dense pass days are Oct 12–13, and the Statue is on the 16th. Go City lists Explorer from $89 (2 choices) up to $299 (10), and All-Inclusive from $169 (1 day) to $414 (10 days). Sales are frequent, so price it the week you buy and compare with buying the six tickets directly (roughly $210–230 per adult at gate prices).

Before you buy: sunset slots at Top of the Rock and Edge sometimes carry an upgrade fee on passes. Check this in the app when you book.
